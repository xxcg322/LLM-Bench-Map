# Interpreting the time windows

Agent, interactive and LLM-scored evaluation become more common in both the
full-year and matched-month comparisons. Individual annual steps need not be
monotonic, and the small 2022 cohort is not a census of early LLM evaluation.

Generated materials rise earlier and remain near half of recent records; the
data do not show sustained recent growth comparable to LLM scoring. Fixed tasks
remain common while fixed-plus-interactive designs grow. These are coexisting
design dimensions, not evidence of wholesale replacement.

English-only shares are broadly stable in full-year comparisons and fluctuate in
matched windows. Language coding limits make small changes secondary observations.
Domain patterns differ: falling shares can coexist with rising absolute counts,
and general/cross-domain labels do not decline monotonically over the entire period.

LLM scoring increases within both agent and non-agent groups. The arithmetic
within-group contribution dominates the agent-composition contribution, but other
changes inside those groups are not ruled out. Joint use of generated materials
and LLM scoring grows without a corresponding increase in lift.

Use `analysis.json` for all counts, shares and common-endpoint comparisons. These
overlapping windows cannot eliminate selection bias or automated coding error.
